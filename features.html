Platform features

Built for builders, tuners, shops, and fans.

Hot Flash is designed around the way car culture actually works: people follow specific builds, ask about mods, compare proof, find trusted tuners, enter shows, and discover sponsors.

Vehicle profiles

Pages for every ride.

Each vehicle gets its own profile, followers, photos, videos, specs, mods, build log, and timeline.

Social garage

One owner, many builds.

User profiles work like garage lobbies, showing every vehicle owned by that builder or shop.

Tuning network

Peer and pro tuning.

Planned tools include tuner listings, compatibility notes, tune requests, datalog reviews, and file history.

Online car shows

Compete from the profile.

Car shows, build battles, public voting, judge scoring, prizes, and trophies can attach directly to vehicles.

Swap meets

Marketplace with context.

Parts and services can connect back to real builds, sellers, shops, and vehicle compatibility.

Sponsorships

Make builds discoverable.

Brands can find active builders, ambassadors, event cars, product testers, and rising projects.
