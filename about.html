About Hot Flash

Social media for the ride—not just the driver.

Hot Flash is a vehicle-first automotive community built around a simple idea: the car should have an identity of its own. Each vehicle can receive a permanent FlashTag, a unique Hot Flash identity connected to its QR profile, photos, videos, modifications, build history and story.

Your garage connects all of your rides to you, while each car remains its own thing. The goal is to give builders, owners, tuners, shops, clubs and fans one organized place to follow real vehicles instead of watching their stories disappear into a feed.

The core idea

Every build gets a permanent identity.

A garage belongs to the owner. A FlashTag belongs to the vehicle. That gives the ride a consistent home for its profile, media, build updates and history through every chapter of the car's life.

From screen to street

A QR people can actually scan.

A FlashTag can be shared online or displayed on the vehicle, letting someone at a meet, show or shop scan the car and land directly on its Hot Flash profile.
