About Hot Flash

Social media for the ride, not just the driver.

Hot Flash is being built as a vehicle-first social tuning platform. A user can own multiple vehicles, but every vehicle can have its own public profile page, followers, gallery, mods, build log, tune history, trophies, and proof of performance.

The goal is to give builders, tuners, shops, clubs, vendors, sponsors, and fans one organized place to connect around real vehicles instead of burying builds inside scattered social feeds.

The core idea

Every build gets an identity.

A garage is the user's collection. A vehicle profile is the star: its own page, followers, activity, media, proof, trophies, and history.
