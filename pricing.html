How the structure works

Creator is a capability, not a separate account type.

Creator, Photographer, Videographer, Media Contributor, Reviewer, Journalist, Builder, Organizer, Mentor, and Ambassador are contribution capabilities assigned to Verified members. A member may hold several without changing memberships.
